WALL HURDLE

Cross the board first. Wall your rival, never shut them in.

Two pawns race to opposite sides. On your turn, move one square or place one wall. Walls slow your rival down, but every pawn must always keep a way through.

Quick rules
  • Goal. Cyan starts at the bottom and must reach any square on the top row. Magenta starts at the top and must reach the bottom row. The first to arrive wins.
  • Your turn. Do exactly one thing: move your pawn one square up, down, left or right, or place one wall. You cannot pass.
  • Walls. Each wall covers two squares of edge. Walls cannot overlap or cross, cannot be moved, and every pawn must keep at least one route to its goal row.
  • Jumps. If the other pawn is next to you with no wall between, jump straight over it. If a wall or the board edge sits behind it, step to either side of it instead.
  • Repeats. If the exact same position appears three times, the game is a draw.

Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter selects, W switches to walls, R turns a wall, Esc cancels.
